# TimeBank

`AGENTS.md` at the repository root is the authoritative guide for every agent
and collaborator: project state, commands, architecture, domain rules,
privacy, and commit conventions. Read it in full before making changes, and
`Docs/README.md` before editing anything under `Docs/`, `Design/` or
`integration-test/*.md`.

Do not copy rules from those files into `.devin/` — a copy goes stale. Change
`AGENTS.md` instead. The two deploy procedures are in `.devin/workflows/`.
